Work on your van.

Already own a van? Bring it to us for a full conversion — or just the bits you want doing. Full and part conversions on your own vehicle, built by hand in our Ruabon workshop.

Full conversions on your van

Bring us your empty or part-finished van and we'll convert it end to end — designed around how you actually travel and built to the exact same standard as the campers we sell. From bare panel van to finished camper, all done in-house.

We sit down with you first to plan the layout, the beds, the kitchen and the finish, then get to work with quality, trusted components throughout.

  • Layout & design around your needs
  • Insulation, ply lining & flooring
  • Pop-top roofs & roof beds (Camperglass)
  • Rock n Roll & drop-down beds
  • Kitchens — sinks, hobs & compressor fridges
  • 12v electrics, leisure batteries, solar & 240v hook-up
  • Eberspächer diesel heating
  • Bespoke furniture, LED lighting & finishing

Part conversions & one-off jobs

Don't need the whole build? We'll do just the parts you want — or pick up and finish a self-build that's stalled.

  • Pop-top roof supply & fit
  • Electrical installs, leisure batteries & solar
  • Diesel heating fitted
  • Beds, seating & furniture
  • Sinks, hobs, fridges & water systems
  • Windows, roof vents & blinds
  • Insulation, ply lining & flooring
  • Snagging, upgrades & repairs to existing conversions
