About Voyager.

A family camper business in Ruabon, North Wales — building and selling campers we'd be proud to travel in ourselves.

Campers, done properly

Voyager Camper Conversions is a bespoke camper builder and dealer based at the Garden Industrial Estate in Ruabon, just outside Wrexham. We design and convert campers by hand, and we sell a hand-picked range of new and used vans ready to drive away.

Whether you want us to build something bespoke around your exact spec, or you'd rather pick a finished camper off our forecourt, we make it easy — with finance, part-exchange and a try-before-you-buy hire scheme to help you get it right.

Every vehicle we sell is HPI checked, and we use trusted brands like Eberspächer, Dometic, Rock n Roll Beds, Streamline and Camperglass throughout our builds. It's the quality we'd want in our own camper.
