Bespoke conversions.

Your van, your way. We design and build every conversion on-site to your exact spec — start with a blank panel van or bring your own.

Built by hand, to your spec

Every Voyager conversion is designed around how you actually want to travel. We sit down with you, work out the layout, the sleeping arrangement, the kitchen and the finish — then build it in our own workshop.

We use trusted, quality components throughout, so your camper is as reliable as it is beautiful.

  • Rock n Roll beds & drop-down beds
  • Pop-top roofs with roof beds (Camperglass)
  • Eberspächer heating
  • Smev sinks & hobs, compressor fridges (Dometic)
  • Leisure batteries, 240v hook-up & battery-to-battery charging
  • Bespoke furniture, LED lighting & Altro flooring

How it works

  • 01 · Chat — tell us your budget, layout and must-haves
  • 02 · Design — we spec the build and the base van
  • 03 · Build — converted by hand in our workshop
  • 04 · Collect — HPI checked, ready for the road
